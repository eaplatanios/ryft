# `ryft-xla-sys`

Low-level `-sys` bindings for [XLA](https://github.com/openxla/xla), MLIR, and PJRT, together with
the artifact-resolution logic and native build wiring used by the rest of the workspace. Most
users do not depend on this crate directly — they get its functionality transitively through
`ryft` and `ryft-pjrt`.
